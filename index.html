Between Life

Ritesh Ravindra Puranik is an author and storyteller fascinated by one enduring subject: people.

His writing moves through human relationships, memory, mystery, psychology and contemporary society, often finding extraordinary stories inside seemingly ordinary lives.

The choices they make. The memories they preserve. The truths they hide. The relationships they leave unfinished.

A Writer's Belief

Humanity Before Drama.

Characters should feel like people—not instruments created merely to move a plot.

Mystery With Meaning.

Suspense becomes powerful when discovering the secret also reveals something about the human beings involved.

Emotion Without Exaggeration.

Some of life's strongest emotions arrive quietly.

Questions Over Easy Answers.

A writer does not always have to tell the reader what to believe.

Stories That Remain.

The most memorable ending may not answer every question. Sometimes it creates another.

Once a book reaches a reader, it no longer belongs entirely to its writer.
